What Blurt does

🎙️

Voice-first conversation

Tap the orb and just talk. No buttons, no forms. Blurt keeps listening — the conversation flows until you say stop.

🧠

Remembers what matters

"Meeting with Sarah tomorrow at 3" becomes a reminder with a heads-up nudge 30 minutes before. "Call mom Friday" — saved.

🫥

Forgets on purpose

Vent about your boss and Blurt acknowledges you — then deliberately forgets. No receipts. No history. Just a friend who listens.

🔊

Speaks like a human

Neural voice via Fish Audio — natural, fast, conversational. Falls back to your device voice when offline.

🔔

Smart reminders

Priority notifications with heads-up banners. Optional 30-minute early nudge. Recurring daily or weekly. Survives reboots.

🔒

Zero secrets. Your keys.

BYOK — paste your own free API keys (Groq, Gemini, Fish). Stored encrypted on-device. The APK ships with nothing.

How it works

1

Tap the orb

Blurt starts listening. No menus, no setup — just press and speak.

2

Speak naturally

Say anything — a reminder, a thought, a vent. Blurt understands context, extracts times, and classifies intent.

3

Blurt replies out loud

A natural voice confirms what it saved. "Done — I'll nudge you at 2:30 pm." The conversation keeps going.

4

It remembers, or it forgets

Important stuff gets saved with smart categories and reminders. Venting? Blurt listens and lets it go. You decide what sticks.

Your data stays yours

Blurt ships zero API keys. You bring your own — stored encrypted in the Android Keystore, never leaving your device. No servers. No tracking. No subscriptions.
